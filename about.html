About

Hey, I'm Brielle. Thanks for stopping by.

My work helps organizations turn complex change, ideas and technology into something people understand and act on.

I study where culture, technology and markets are heading, then build the tools, systems and stories leaders need to grow with them. I've led research, campaigns and programs that brought new technology and new ways of working to people around the world, with Google, the Tony Blair Institute, Art Works For Change, Workwell and more.

I spent eight years at Google in San Francisco, Singapore and beyond, across insights, partner marketing and product marketing for Assistant, Maps and Google for Startups. I then led digital inclusion partnerships at the Tony Blair Institute. Since 2020 I've run Strouse House Studio, an advisory practice for early-stage and transforming organizations working on AI, go-to-market strategy, partnerships and culture.

I'm most interested in new models of work, living and education that let more people shape their own futures. I suspect that's why I keep ending up in rooms where something new is being built and someone needs to ask who it's for.
